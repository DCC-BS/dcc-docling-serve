PDF → Markdown converter comparison

Key findings

    Total conversion time

    Sum over the documents every tool converted successfully, models already loaded. Hover for failures.

    Seconds per page

    Median over documents of conversion time divided by page count.

    Ground-truth quality (synthetic documents)

    Mean over the 4 synthetic PDFs. 1.00 = perfect. Noise = share of output words that are not in the document.

    Where the text comes from (synthetic documents)

    Share of the words of each text source found in the output. Only OCR can recover outlined, image and scanned text.

    Coverage of the PDF text layer (real documents)

    Word recall and precision against the PDF's own text, over pages that have one. Recall below 1 means missing text; precision below 1 means extra words (OCR of images, noise, duplicates).

    Startup and model loading

    Time before the first document can be converted (container start or model load) plus the first, cold conversion.

    Strengths and weaknesses per tool

    Drawn from the scores above and from reading the outputs side by side in the Compare tab.

    Per document

    Each cell: conversion time and word recall against the PDF text layer (or the ground truth for synthetic documents). Cell colour = recall. Click a row to compare outputs.
